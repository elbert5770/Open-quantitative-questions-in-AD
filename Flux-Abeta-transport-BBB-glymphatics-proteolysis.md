# BBB transport

[doi:10.1002/ana.24270] The plasma V-A difference across the brain multiplied by cerebral blood flow rate (Qcerebral) yields the rate of efflux of Ab peptides out of the brain.  V-A difference x Q cerebral = 9.7 ng/min  Based on this approximation, all processes that result in transfer of Ab peptides from the brain into the central veins sum to a rate of mass transfer of 9.7ng/min.

# Total loss of Abeta
[doi:10.1002/ana.24270] Using stable isotope labeling kinetics, we recently reported a fractional turnover rate of 0.14/h21 for total irreversible loss of soluble Ab in the CNS of normal controls
