# Previous work

[doi: 10.1007/s00401-020-02175-1] Cored plaques and diffuse plaques of similar sizes gave integrated intensities of 9 ± 2.3 and 2.8 ± 3.1, respectively, suggesting that in vivo diffuse plaques would need to be present at three times the frequency of the cored  plaques of similar size to give comparable [18F]flutemetamol signal.
